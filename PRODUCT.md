# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mixed audience: CTOs and engineering managers evaluating a senior hire, companies assessing a consultant for enterprise AI/systems work, and peer engineers in the same craft space. They arrive via LinkedIn, GitHub, or direct referral. Context is professional evaluation: they want to quickly gauge seniority, domain depth, and whether this person is serious.

## Product Purpose

Personal brand surface for Artem Polovyi, enterprise engineer in Zurich. A decade of systems work at Audi, Infineon, UBS, Flowable, and PEAX, now focused on making AI reliable in production. The site exists to convert a name into an impression: senior, precise, substantive. Success is a visitor who leaves thinking "this person knows what they're doing" within five seconds.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, heavy. Like a machined steel instrument. Confidence through density and restraint, not through spectacle or decoration. The site doesn't perform expertise; it simply presents it. Every element earns its place or gets removed.

## Anti-references

- **Generic dev portfolio**: dark mode + terminal font + GitHub contribution graphs + project card grids. Over-indexed on developer identity signaling.
- **SaaS landing page**: gradient blobs, feature grids, "trusted by" logo bars, CTAs everywhere. Corporate-template energy.
- **Overly minimal / empty**: white page with tiny text that feels unfinished or pretentious rather than restrained. Minimalism without substance.
- **Flashy / animated**: scroll-jacking, particle effects, 3D WebGL scenes. Spectacle over substance.

## Design Principles

1. **Authority through restraint**: every element earns its space. Nothing decorative, nothing performative. The absence of noise IS the signal.
2. **Density carries weight**: the impression of substance comes from precision and deliberate compression, not from sprawl or generous whitespace alone.
3. **Substance over spectacle**: no animation, effect, or visual trick that doesn't directly communicate competence.
4. **Only what's real**: no invented metrics, no filler sections, no "about me" prose that restates the obvious. If it can't be verified, it doesn't belong.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reasonable defaults: sufficient contrast ratios, keyboard navigable, screen-reader friendly, respects prefers-reduced-motion. No specific WCAG certification target.
